## Title: I remember you!
## APIVersion: 101050 101051
## Version: 0.4.0
## AddOnVersion: 400
## SavedVariables: IRememberYouSV
## Description: Remembers players across @account and character renames. Shows a stable name in the reticle target frame and in chat, links old and new account IDs into one player entity, adds a resizable browsable player list, and gives each player one icon on every surface, including 3D icons above group members through OdySupportIcons and an optional marker above the reticle.
